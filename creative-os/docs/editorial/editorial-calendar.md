# Purpose

The Editorial Calendar establishes the recurring publishing rhythm for Campfire Horror Tales.

Rather than posting content randomly, the Editorial Calendar provides a structured schedule that balances original fiction, educational content, community engagement, and behind-the-scenes storytelling.

The calendar is designed to create a consistent reader experience while supporting the long-term Editorial Publishing System.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Every week combines original storytelling with authentic research, creative reflections, and atmospheric inspiration.

Each editorial series has a unique purpose while contributing to a cohesive publishing experience.

The schedule should remain consistent whenever possible while allowing flexibility for special releases, seasonal content, and major publishing events.

---

# Weekly Editorial Schedule

## Saturday

### 📖 Story Saturday

**Purpose**

Feature one original Campfire Horror Tale.

Story Saturday is the cornerstone of the editorial calendar and introduces readers to new stories, characters, and mysteries from the world of Dawn Hill.

**Typical Content**

- Story announcement
- Instagram cover artwork
- Website link
- Short teaser
- Call to action

---

## Sunday

### 👻 Ghost Story Sunday

**Purpose**

Share authentic ghost stories, haunted locations, unexplained mysteries, and historical accounts that complement the atmosphere of Campfire Horror Tales.

These posts educate readers while encouraging discussion and curiosity.

**Typical Content**

- Historical ghost stories
- Haunted places
- Local legends
- Paranormal folklore
- Community questions

---

## Monday

### 🔥 Behind the Fire

**Purpose**

Reveal the real-world inspiration behind Campfire Horror Tales.

Behind the Fire documents the creative journey by connecting original stories with the experiences, photographs, locations, memories, and research that inspired them.

This editorial series strengthens the relationship between author and reader by demonstrating how reality becomes fiction.

**Typical Content**

- Original photography
- Writing reflections
- Research notes
- Creative process
- Personal inspiration
- Artwork

---

## Wednesday

### 🌲 Quebec Spotlight

**Purpose**

Celebrate the landscapes, history, and atmosphere of Quebec that inspire the fictional world of Campfire Horror Tales.

Whenever possible, original photography should be used.

**Typical Content**

- Forests
- Lakes
- Small villages
- Historic buildings
- Misty landscapes
- Seasonal beauty

---

## Friday

### 📚 Folklore Friday

**Purpose**

Introduce readers to authentic folklore, legends, myths, traditions, and historical mysteries from Quebec and Canada.

These posts strengthen the world-building behind Campfire Horror Tales while encouraging readers to explore the cultural roots that inspire future fiction.

**Typical Content**

- Legends
- Folk creatures
- Historical traditions
- Regional myths
- Cultural history

---

# Editorial Priorities

The Editorial Calendar is built upon five permanent content pillars.

- Original Stories
- Ghost Story Sunday
- Behind the Fire
- Quebec Spotlight
- Folklore Friday

Every recurring post should support one of these pillars.

---

# Flexibility

The Editorial Calendar serves as the standard publishing rhythm rather than a rigid schedule.

Special events may temporarily replace or supplement scheduled content, including:

- New story releases
- Seasonal publishing
- Halloween events
- Book launches
- Author announcements
- Community milestones

---

# Future Growth

As Tree Wishes Publishing expands, additional editorial series may be introduced while preserving the overall publishing rhythm.

Any new recurring series should have:

- A defined purpose
- A consistent publishing cadence
- Editorial standards
- Visual identity
- Supporting workflow documentation
