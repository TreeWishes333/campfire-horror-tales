## Purpose

The Research Library is the long-term knowledge archive for Campfire Horror Tales.

It collects the real places, legends, folklore, history, creatures, and cultural traditions that inspire the fictional world of Saint-Clair-des-Bois.

Unlike the publishing documentation, this section contains creative reference material rather than workflow instructions.

## Research Philosophy

The Research Library serves as the creative foundation for Campfire Horror Tales.

Its purpose is to collect authentic places, folklore, legends, history, personal experiences, photography, and cultural references that inspire original fiction.

Research is gathered to deepen understanding and spark creativity—not to reproduce existing stories.

Whenever possible, primary sources, original photography, firsthand experiences, and reputable historical references should be preferred over secondary summaries.

Every research entry should contribute to one or more areas of the Editorial Publishing System, including:

- Story development
- Ghost Story Sunday
- Quebec Spotlight
- Folklore Friday
- Behind the Fire
- Future manuscripts

---

## Guiding Principle

Research serves inspiration—not imitation.

Campfire Horror Tales is built on original fiction.

Real locations, history, folklore, photography, and personal experiences become creative inspiration that is transformed into new stories.
