## Summary

Long before Mont Tremblant became one of Canada's most recognizable mountain landscapes, Indigenous peoples regarded it as a sacred place inhabited by powerful spirits.

According to traditional legend, the mountain would tremble whenever the balance between humanity and nature was disturbed. The story reminds us that the land itself deserves respect and that the natural world possesses a spirit beyond human understanding.

Today, the mountain continues to inspire visitors, storytellers, and those drawn to the mystery of Quebec's forests.

---

## Historical Background

The legend originates from Indigenous oral traditions associated with the Laurentians.

Campfire Horror Tales presents this story respectfully as part of Quebec's cultural heritage, recognizing that Indigenous stories belong to living traditions and should never be sensationalized or treated as fictional inventions.

---

## Why it Inspires Campfire Horror Tales

The forests surrounding Mont Tremblant capture the quiet mystery that inspires the fictional world of Saint-Clair-des-Bois.

Ancient forests.

Hidden roads.

Morning mist.

Silence.

Places where imagination naturally begins to wonder what might still live among the trees.
