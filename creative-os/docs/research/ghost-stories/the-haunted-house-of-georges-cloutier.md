### Québec Ghost Story: The Haunted House of Georges Cloutier

Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain, Laurentians, Québec

In 1922, Catherine Longpré was alone at home when something strange began happening around the small house she shared with her husband, Georges Cloutier.

As evening fell, the family's two horses suddenly became terrified, jumping and neighing as though something unseen had frightened them.

Then Catherine discovered something even stranger.

The firewood normally stacked beneath the stove had been moved onto the bed in the room where her mother-in-law, Julie Prézeault, had died.

Catherine was frightened enough to seek help from her neighbour.

That night, two young boys stayed with her.

But they would not sleep peacefully for long.

In the darkness, the children awoke to the sound of an empty rocking chair moving back and forth on its own.

Then the flame of the oil lamp began rising and falling, as though someone were turning the wick.

Was the house haunted by the spirit of Catherine's mother-in-law?

Or was something else disturbing the family?

The strange events were preserved in the local history of Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain, in an account recorded by the Société d'histoire et de généalogie des Hautes-Laurentides.

And the story became known as The Haunted House of Georges Cloutier.
