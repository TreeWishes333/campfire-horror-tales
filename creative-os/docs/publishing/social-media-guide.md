# Purpose

The Social Media Guide defines the editorial standards, content strategy, and communication principles used across all Campfire Horror Tales social media channels.

Rather than serving as advertising, social media extends the storytelling experience by introducing readers to the world of Dawn Hill, the landscapes of Quebec, authentic folklore, and the creative process behind every story.

Social media exists to build a community of readers who enjoy mystery, folklore, horror, and storytelling.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of social media are to:

- Introduce readers to Campfire Horror Tales.
- Build anticipation for new stories.
- Encourage readers to visit the website.
- Share authentic folklore and history.
- Reveal the creative process behind the stories.
- Build a recognizable publishing brand.
- Create long-term engagement rather than short-term promotion.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Every social media post should provide value.

Rather than asking readers to purchase or consume content, each post should educate, inspire curiosity, or deepen their connection to the fictional world of Campfire Horror Tales.

Promotion is a natural outcome of consistent storytelling—not the primary objective.

---

# Content Pillars

All published content should belong to one of the following editorial pillars.

## Original Stories

Purpose

Introduce readers to new Campfire Horror Tales through Story Saturday.

Typical Content

- Story announcements
- Story artwork
- Quotes
- Teasers
- Character introductions

---

## Ghost Story Sunday

Purpose

Share authentic ghost stories, haunted places, unexplained mysteries, and historical accounts that complement the themes of Campfire Horror Tales.

Typical Content

- Haunted locations
- Historical mysteries
- Local ghost stories
- Community discussion

---

## Behind the Fire

Purpose

Reveal the real-world inspiration behind each story.

Typical Content

- Original photography
- Writing reflections
- Locations
- Research
- Sketches
- Creative process

---

## Quebec Spotlight

Purpose

Celebrate the landscapes, forests, villages, lakes, and atmosphere that inspire the fictional world of Dawn Hill.

Whenever possible, use original photography.

Typical Content

- Lakes
- Forests
- Mist
- Seasonal landscapes
- Historic villages
- Quiet places

---

## Folklore Friday

Purpose

Introduce readers to authentic folklore, myths, legends, traditions, and mysterious history from Quebec and Canada.

Typical Content

- Folk legends
- Mythical creatures
- Traditions
- Historical folklore
- Cultural heritage

---

# Editorial Voice

Campfire Horror Tales should always communicate with readers using a voice that is:

- Atmospheric
- Curious
- Thoughtful
- Respectful
- Authentic
- Imaginative

Posts should invite readers into the mystery rather than rely on sensationalism or shock.

---

# Visual Identity

Images should maintain a consistent visual style across all platforms.

Characteristics include:

- Cinematic lighting
- Natural environments
- Forests and lakes
- Seasonal atmosphere
- Mist and fog
- Warm campfire tones
- Minimal text
- Consistent typography

Platform-specific design standards are documented separately.

---

# Calls to Action

Every post should include an appropriate invitation for readers to engage.

Examples include:

- Read the full story.
- Share your thoughts.
- Have you ever experienced something similar?
- What would you do?
- Which story should readers discover next?
- Follow for future Campfire Horror Tales.

Calls to action should encourage conversation rather than sales.

---

# Posting Frequency

The standard editorial schedule follows:

- Saturday — Story Saturday
- Sunday — Ghost Story Sunday
- Monday — Behind the Fire
- Wednesday — Quebec Spotlight
- Friday — Folklore Friday

The schedule may be adjusted for major story releases or seasonal events.

---

# Hashtag Strategy

Hashtags should be relevant, concise, and support discoverability.

Typical categories include:

- Horror
- Ghost stories
- Campfire stories
- Quebec
- Canadian folklore
- Writing
- Storytelling
- Reading
- Autumn
- Halloween

Platform-specific hashtag lists may evolve over time.

---

# Community Engagement

Community interaction is part of the editorial experience.

Whenever practical:

- Respond to comments.
- Encourage discussion.
- Thank readers.
- Ask thoughtful questions.
- Build genuine relationships.

Community should be treated as an extension of storytelling rather than an audience to market to.

---

# Success Metrics

Performance should be evaluated using meaningful engagement rather than vanity metrics.

Examples include:

- Story reads
- Website visits
- Saves
- Shares
- Comments
- Follows
- Newsletter sign-ups
- Returning readers

Metrics should inform future decisions without replacing editorial judgment.

---

# Future Automation

Future automation may assist with:

- Caption drafts
- Publishing schedules
- Hashtag suggestions
- Image generation
- Editorial reminders
- Analytics summaries

All generated content remains subject to editorial review and approval.
