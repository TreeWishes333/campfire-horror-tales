# The Haunted House of Georges Cloutier

## Opening Hook

In 1922, an empty rocking chair began moving in a small house in Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain.

## Narration Script

In 1922, Catherine Longpré was alone at the small house she shared with her husband, Georges Cloutier, when their two horses suddenly became terrified.

Then she found the firewood from beneath the stove piled onto a bed in the room where her mother-in-law, Julie Prézeault, had died.

Frightened, Catherine asked a neighbour for help. That night, two young boys stayed in the house with her.

But in the darkness, they awoke to an empty rocking chair moving slowly back and forth on its own.

Then the flame of the oil lamp began rising and falling, as though an unseen hand were turning the wick.

The account was preserved in the local history of Notre-Dame-de-Pontmain.

Was it the spirit of Julie Prézeault? Or something the family could never explain?
