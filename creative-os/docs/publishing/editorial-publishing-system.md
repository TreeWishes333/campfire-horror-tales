**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-01

# Purpose

The Editorial Publishing System defines the operational framework used by Tree Wishes Publishing to transform original creative works into coordinated publishing campaigns.

It establishes the standards, workflows, editorial practices, and review processes that guide every stage of publication, from the completion of a manuscript through website publication, social media, and future distribution channels.

This document serves as the governing specification for all publishing activities within the Campfire Horror Tales project and provides the foundation for future AI-assisted automation.

---

# Scope

The Editorial Publishing System applies to all original content produced under Tree Wishes Publishing.

It defines:

- Editorial philosophy
- Publishing workflows
- Content lifecycle
- Creative review process
- Publishing standards
- AI-assisted publishing principles
- Future automation requirements

Supporting implementation details are documented separately within Creative OS.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

Tree Wishes Publishing is built upon original storytelling.

Every story begins with imagination, personal experience, research, photography, observations, memories, and creative writing.

Technology supports the publishing process but never replaces the creative act.

Automation exists to reduce repetitive work while preserving originality, authenticity, and editorial quality.

The manuscript remains the single source of creative truth.

---

# The Source of Truth

Creative OS is the operational knowledge base for the Editorial Publishing System.

All publishing standards, editorial decisions, workflows, and automation specifications are documented within Creative OS.

Future AI assistants and automation tools should reference these documents rather than relying on undocumented prompts or ad hoc processes.

---

# Publishing Lifecycle

Every completed manuscript progresses through a consistent editorial lifecycle.

```text
Story Concept

↓

Research

↓

Writing

↓

Editing

↓

Final Manuscript

↓

Website Publication

↓

Editorial Publishing Campaign

↓

Review & Approval

↓

Publication

↓

Archive & Continuous Learning
```

The completed manuscript serves as the foundation for every publishing asset that follows.

---

# Editorial Workflow

Each completed story may generate multiple coordinated publishing assets, including:

- Website publication
- Story artwork
- Story Saturday feature
- Ghost Story Sunday
- Behind the Fire
- Quebec Spotlight
- Folklore Friday
- Newsletter content
- Pinterest content
- Future publishing platforms

Every asset originates from the same manuscript to ensure consistency across the publishing ecosystem.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Author

Creates the original story and remains the final creative authority.

## Creative OS

Provides the documented standards, workflows, and publishing guidance.

## AI Assistants

Assist with organization, drafting, formatting, editing support, brainstorming, research assistance, and automation.

AI does not replace creative authorship or editorial judgment.

## Automation

Implements documented workflows after they have been designed, reviewed, and approved.

---

# AI-Assisted Publishing Principles

AI should:

- Reduce repetitive work.
- Improve consistency.
- Support editorial quality.
- Assist with research.
- Generate draft publishing assets.
- Preserve the author's voice.

AI should never become the origin of original fiction or replace editorial review.

---

# Review and Approval Process

No content is published automatically.

Every publishing asset progresses through the following workflow:

```text
Generated

↓

Needs Review

↓

Edited

↓

Approved

↓

Scheduled

↓

Published

↓

Archived
```

The author remains responsible for final approval.

---

# Quality Standards

Every publishing asset should:

- Support the Campfire Horror Tales brand.
- Reflect the documented editorial standards.
- Maintain a consistent visual identity.
- Preserve the author's voice.
- Be factually accurate where applicable.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Deliver value to readers.

---

# Future Vision

The Editorial Publishing System is designed to evolve alongside Tree Wishes Publishing.

Campfire Horror Tales serves as the first implementation of this publishing framework.

Over time, additional publishing brands may adopt the same operational model while maintaining their own editorial voices, audiences, and visual identities.

Creative OS will continue to serve as the source of truth for both human collaborators and future AI-assisted publishing automation.
