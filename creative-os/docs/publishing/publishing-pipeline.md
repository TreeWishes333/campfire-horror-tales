# Purpose

The Publishing Pipeline defines the repeatable workflow that transforms a completed manuscript into a coordinated publishing campaign.

Rather than creating individual pieces of content independently, every publishing asset is derived from the completed manuscript. This ensures consistency across platforms while preserving the author's creative voice.

The Publishing Pipeline serves as the operational blueprint for future publishing automation.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every completed manuscript is the single source of creative truth.

All publishing assets should originate from the approved manuscript rather than being developed independently.

---

# Publishing Workflow

Every story progresses through the following stages.

```text
Story Concept

↓

Research

↓

Writing

↓

Editing

↓

Final Manuscript

↓

Website Publication

↓

Editorial Publishing Campaign

↓

Review & Approval

↓

Publication

↓

Performance Review

↓

Archive & Continuous Learning
```

---

# Publishing Campaign

Once a manuscript is approved, the Editorial Publishing System generates a coordinated publishing campaign.

Campaign assets may include:

## Website

- Story page
- Hero artwork
- Story metadata
- Reader navigation

---

## Story Saturday

The featured weekly story announcement.

Includes:

- Instagram artwork
- Caption
- Website link
- Call to action

---

## Ghost Story Sunday

A related historical ghost story, haunting, mystery, or unexplained event that complements the atmosphere of the featured story.

Purpose:

- Education
- Reader engagement
- Community discussion

---

## Behind the Fire

A behind-the-scenes reflection documenting the real-world inspiration behind the story.

Possible sources include:

- Original photography
- Locations
- Memories
- Research
- Writing reflections
- Creative process
- Artwork

Purpose:

Reveal how reality became fiction.

---

## Quebec Spotlight

Highlights atmospheric locations from Quebec that inspire the fictional world of Campfire Horror Tales.

Priority is given to original photography whenever possible.

Purpose:

Connect readers with the landscapes that inspire the stories.

---

## Folklore Friday

Share authentic folklore, legends, traditions, and historical mysteries from Quebec and Canada.

Purpose:

Educate readers while strengthening the world-building behind Campfire Horror Tales.

---

## Newsletter

Optional expanded content including:

- Story announcement
- Behind the Fire
- Writing updates
- Upcoming releases

# Editorial Review

Every publishing asset follows the same review workflow.

```text
Generated

↓

Needs Review

↓

Edited

↓

Approved

↓

Scheduled

↓

Published

↓

Archived
```

Publication never occurs without editorial approval.

---

# Continuous Learning

Following publication, the Editorial Publishing System records lessons learned from each release.

Examples include:

- Reader engagement
- Popular topics
- Successful imagery
- Story performance
- Editorial improvements
- Publishing workflow refinements

These observations inform future publishing decisions while preserving the author's creative vision.
