## Every story has a spark. Here's the one that started this tale.

At a summer fair in Québec, I came across a little boy selling homemade tin-can telephones, and an old Raggedy Ann doll with a handwritten sign. $1.

I paid with a $5 bill. He counted out all of his change into my hand, and only later did I realize I had taken every last coin he had.

I went back to find him, but he was gone. I never saw him again.

The doll came home with me. Friends and family noticed right away that she had a strange, unsettling presence. But I couldn't get rid of it. It felt as though the boy had passed her to me, and she was mine to keep.

She had a connection to this place.
She belonged in these woods...
until she chose her next transaction.

Some childhood treasures never really leave you.
