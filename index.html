Behind the Fire

I found the mask tucked away in our cottage shortly after we bought it in 2019. It felt as though it had been waiting there for someone to discover it.

Some people thought it was unsettling. I couldn’t stop wondering who had left it behind... or why.

It became the spark that lit Campfire Horror Tales.

The mask is real. Everything that followed grew from that first discovery.

Behind the Fire

The frog in this photograph lives in my own backyard garden.

The moment I took the picture, I was suddenly transported back to childhood.

There was a boy who used to wander the bog near our home. My father would become furious when he saw him tormenting the frogs.

After he left, my brothers, sisters, and I would quietly return to rescue the ones we could.

Memory has a strange way of finding us. Sometimes it begins with nothing more than the eyes of a frog watching from the reeds.
