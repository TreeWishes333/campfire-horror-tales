MEET DAWN HILL

MEET DAWN HILL

Dawn Hill is the kind of storyteller who listens for the hush between the trees, the soft crackle of a campfire, the stillness of a lake at dusk, and the old folklore that seems to linger in the dark. These stories are shaped by family memories, lonely forests, and the beautiful, haunting feeling that something ancient is waiting just beyond the light. Campfire Horror Tales is the first collection of these stories, with additional folklore and future collections planned.
